# Mandatory Clause Library | مكتبة البنود الإلزامية

Reference checklist for `legal-counsel`. Minimums are **board defaults**, set before negotiation. Change them by
board resolution only. Percentages are of contract price unless stated. These are drafting standards, not legal
advice. Licensed counsel in the governing law jurisdiction must review the final text.

## A. Equipment supply contract (G2)

| ID | Clause | Minimum standard | Severity if missing |
|---|---|---|---|
| EQ-01 | Scope and incorporation of locked spec | `specs/rfp_spec_v1.md` incorporated by reference with precedence over vendor documents | CRITICAL |
| EQ-02 | Order of precedence | Contract body > technical annex (locked spec) > vendor offer | CRITICAL |
| EQ-03 | Foundation drawings and load data delivery | Delivered within N weeks of contract effectiveness. LD per week of delay. This is the project critical path | CRITICAL |
| EQ-04 | Delivery schedule LD (الشروط الجزائية للتأخير) | 0.5% per week, cap 10% | CRITICAL |
| EQ-05 | Performance guarantees | Output t/h, scrap %, energy per ton, dimensional tolerance standard. LD per shortfall unit, cap, and rejection right below a floor | CRITICAL |
| EQ-06 | Advance payment guarantee | 100% of advance, from a bank acceptable under current banking constraints, valid until delivery + 30 days | CRITICAL |
| EQ-07 | Performance bond (ضمان حسن التنفيذ) | 10%, valid until final acceptance + warranty transition | CRITICAL |
| EQ-08 | Retention | 5 to 10% until final acceptance, released against warranty bond | HIGH |
| EQ-09 | Payment milestones | Tied to verifiable events (documents, FAT, delivery, SAT, final acceptance), never to dates alone | HIGH |
| EQ-10 | INCOTERMS 2020 | Named term and named place (e.g. CIF Latakia). Insurance 110% of value, all risks | HIGH |
| EQ-11 | FAT at vendor works | Buyer witness right, protocol attached, failed FAT stops shipment | HIGH |
| EQ-12 | SAT and commissioning | Test protocol, duration, trial run tonnage, acceptance criteria | CRITICAL |
| EQ-13 | Installation supervision | Expert count, days, daily rate for overruns caused by buyer only, vendor bears own delays | HIGH |
| EQ-14 | Training | Hours per discipline, on site during installation, named trainees, sign off sheets | HIGH |
| EQ-15 | Deliverables | Full electrical drawings, hydraulic schematics, PLC and HMI source, parameter lists, manuals (EN or AR), offline licences | CRITICAL |
| EQ-16 | Spare parts | 24 month commissioning and operating spares package itemized with part numbers. Parts availability 10 years. Price list validity | CRITICAL |
| EQ-17 | Warranty | 12 to 24 months from final acceptance, response time, on site repair cost | HIGH |
| EQ-18 | Sanctions and export control | Vendor warrants export licences. Substitution with equivalent serviceable brands at no extra cost. Buyer termination without penalty and refund of advance if delivery is blocked | CRITICAL |
| EQ-19 | Force majeure | Excludes sanctions and currency controls known at signing. Notice within 14 days. Termination after 120 days | HIGH |
| EQ-20 | Dispute resolution | Institutional arbitration (e.g. ICC, DIAC), neutral seat, English language, enforceable against vendor assets | HIGH |
| EQ-21 | Governing law | Named neutral law, consistent with seat | MEDIUM |
| EQ-22 | Liability cap and exclusions | Cap not below contract price. Carve outs for gross negligence, IP, confidentiality | MEDIUM |
| EQ-23 | Title and risk | Title on payment or delivery, risk per INCOTERMS | MEDIUM |
| EQ-24 | Claims and notice | Shortage and damage claim window at least 30 days after receipt at site | HIGH |
| EQ-25 | Anti corruption and compliance | Standard | LOW |

## B. Civil works contract (G3)

| ID | Clause | Minimum standard | Severity if missing |
|---|---|---|---|
| CV-01 | Drawings | Vendor foundation drawings incorporated by revision number. Contractor must not deviate | CRITICAL |
| CV-02 | Milestones with LD | Foundations, hangar closed, substation civil. LD per day, cap | CRITICAL |
| CV-03 | Concrete quality | Grade, cube tests by an independent lab, curing time before loading | CRITICAL |
| CV-04 | Anchor bolt tolerances | Per vendor drawing, surveyed and signed before pouring | CRITICAL |
| CV-05 | Price indexation | SYP contracts: index or FX adjustment formula with a cap | HIGH |
| CV-06 | Performance bond and retention | 10% bond or retention, defects liability 12 months | HIGH |
| CV-07 | Variation orders | Written, priced, approved before execution | HIGH |
| CV-08 | HSE | Site safety plan, insurance, contractor liability | HIGH |
| CV-09 | Dispute resolution | Local courts or arbitration, stated | MEDIUM |

## C. Freight and clearing (G3 and G4)

| ID | Clause | Minimum standard | Severity if missing |
|---|---|---|---|
| FR-01 | Free days | Stated free days at port and for container return | HIGH |
| FR-02 | Demurrage and detention rates | Stated per day per container | HIGH |
| FR-03 | Heavy lift responsibility | Who provides cranes and low beds, at what cost | HIGH |
| FR-04 | Insurance | Door to site or port to site coverage stated | HIGH |
| FR-05 | Clearing agent liability | Errors in declarations, fines, delays caused by agent | MEDIUM |
